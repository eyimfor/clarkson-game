Calculate 1996 Accounts Payable

First, calculate Purchases:

Purchases = ΔInventory + COGS
ΔInventory = Inventory(1996) - Inventory(1995)

Then calculate AP:

AP = (AP Days on Hand) × (Purchases / 365)

Given:

  • Inventory(1995) = $587
  • Inventory(1996) = $671
  • 1996 COGS = $4,125
  • AP Days on Hand = 10 days
🧮 Calculate 1996 Accounts Payable in thousands:
Hint: Purchases = ($671 - $587) + $4,125 = $4,209. Then AP = 10 × ($4,209 / 365) ≈ ?

STEP 16 OF 16
Understanding the Complete Valuation

🎯 Putting It All Together:

You've now mastered calculating Free Cash Flow for Year 1! In a complete valuation, you would:


  1. Project FCF for all forecast years (1996-2000)
  2. Calculate PV of those FCFs (discounting at WACC = 11.5%)
  3. Calculate Terminal Value beyond 2000
  4. Sum to get Enterprise Value (EV)
  5. Add Non-Operating Assets and subtract Debt to get Market Value of Equity

The complete formula:

Market Value of Equity = Enterprise Value + Non-Operating Assets - Debt
= $2,014 + $56 - $737 = $1,333
